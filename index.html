Introduction

I am an applied researcher focusing on criminal justice and the broader public sector. My work centers on mixed-methods research, program evaluation, and policy analysis to evaluate and improve local and state systems. Currently, I am a Senior Research Assistant at Portland State University’s Center for Public Service and am a PhD Candidate at Washington State University.

I am based in Portland, Oregon, where I live with my dog, Whiskey. Having been present for every academic milestone from my Master’s program through my PhD, it feels only right to include him here.

Current Projects

Criminal Justice

  • Treatment Courts: Evaluation of phase use across adult treatment drug courts in the US
  • Pretrial Justice: Examining the relationship between failure to appear and monetary release in Oregon
  • Social Supports: Examining the effect of visitation on recidivism and misconduct for incarcerated individuals

Public Sector

  • Elections: Survey of local election officials and examination of public confidence in elections
  • Mass Care Planning: Evaluation and technical assistance for the Oregon Department of Human Services
  • Public Engagement: Developing strategic frameworks and engagement plans for the Oregon Department of Transportation's Transportation Safety Office
